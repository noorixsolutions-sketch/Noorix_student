# English First Language — 5_IGCSE_Upper_Secondary

> Develops reading, writing, speaking, and listening skills in English for first-language learners.

**Syllabus code:** `0500`  
**Age range:** 14–16  
**Prerequisites:** Strong command of English at primary level.

## Contents of this folder
- [`Syllabus/`](./Syllabus/) — official syllabus overview and topic list
- [`Notes/`](./Notes/) — structured study guide and revision plan
- [`Past_Papers/`](./Past_Papers/) — index of past-paper resources
- [`Mark_Schemes/`](./Mark_Schemes/) — index of mark schemes & examiner reports
- [`Resources/`](./Resources/) — textbooks, videos, and online tools

## Key Topics
1. Reading — comprehension, analysis, summary
1. Writing — directed, discursive, descriptive, narrative
1. Speaking and listening (where assessed)
1. Grammar, punctuation, and vocabulary
