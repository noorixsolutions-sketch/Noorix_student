# Additional Mathematics — 5_IGCSE_Upper_Secondary

> Stronger emphasis on algebraic manipulation, functions, and calculus than IGCSE Mathematics.

**Syllabus code:** `0606`  
**Age range:** 15–16  
**Prerequisites:** IGCSE Mathematics (or taken concurrently). Designed for high-attaining students.

## Key Topics
1. Functions — domain, range, compositions, inverses
1. Quadratic functions and discriminant
1. Indices, surds, and logarithms
1. Simultaneous equations
1. Polynomials — factor theorem, remainder theorem
1. Trigonometric identities and equations
1. Differentiation and integration
1. Vectors in 2D
1. Kinematics
