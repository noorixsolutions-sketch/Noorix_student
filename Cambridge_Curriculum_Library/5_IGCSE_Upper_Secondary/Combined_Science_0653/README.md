# Combined Science — 5_IGCSE_Upper_Secondary

> A double-award IGCSE integrating Biology, Chemistry, and Physics at a slightly reduced depth than the separate sciences.

**Syllabus code:** `0653`  
**Age range:** 14–16  
**Prerequisites:** Lower Secondary Science.

## Contents of this folder
- [`Syllabus/`](./Syllabus/) — official syllabus overview and topic list
- [`Notes/`](./Notes/) — structured study guide and revision plan
- [`Past_Papers/`](./Past_Papers/) — index of past-paper resources
- [`Mark_Schemes/`](./Mark_Schemes/) — index of mark schemes & examiner reports
- [`Resources/`](./Resources/) — textbooks, videos, and online tools

## Key Topics
1. Biology: cells, nutrition, reproduction, ecology
1. Chemistry: atoms, bonding, reactions, organic intro
1. Physics: forces, energy, waves, electricity
