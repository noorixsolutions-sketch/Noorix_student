# Chemistry — 5_IGCSE_Upper_Secondary

> Covers particulate nature of matter, atomic structure, bonding, reactions, acids/bases, metals, organic chemistry, and electrochemistry.

**Syllabus code:** `0620`  
**Age range:** 14–16  
**Prerequisites:** Lower Secondary Science.

## Key Topics
1. States of matter and diffusion
1. Atoms, elements, and compounds
1. Stoichiometry and the mole concept
1. Chemical bonding and structure
1. Acids, bases, and salts
1. Reactivity and extraction of metals
1. Rates of reaction and equilibrium
1. Organic chemistry
1. Electrochemistry
