### Week 1: The life of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH)
**Learning Objectives:**
- - Define and explain key terminology related to **The life of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH)**- - Describe the fundamental principles and concepts of **The life of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH)**- - Apply understanding of **The life of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH)** to solve problems and analyse scenarios- - Evaluate and critique different perspectives or approaches within **The life of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH)**- - Make connections between **The life of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH)** and other areas of the subject
