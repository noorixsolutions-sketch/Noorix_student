# Biology — 5_IGCSE_Upper_Secondary

> Covers cell biology, enzymes, nutrition, transport, respiration, coordination, reproduction, inheritance, ecology, and human influences.

**Syllabus code:** `0610`  
**Age range:** 14–16  
**Prerequisites:** Lower Secondary Science.

## Key Topics
1. Characteristics and classification of living organisms
1. Organisation of the organism (cells, tissues)
1. Movement in and out of cells
1. Enzymes and biological molecules
1. Plant nutrition and transport
1. Animal nutrition, respiration, and circulation
1. Coordination, response, and homeostasis
1. Reproduction and inheritance
1. Ecology and the environment
1. Use of biological resources
