# Physics — 5_IGCSE_Upper_Secondary

> Introduces classical and modern physics: motion, forces, energy, waves, electricity, magnetism and atomic physics for Cambridge IGCSE.

**Syllabus code:** `0625`  
**Age range:** 14–16  
**Prerequisites:** Lower Secondary Science with strong mathematical fluency.

## Key Topics
1. Measurement and units — SI units, vernier callipers, micrometer, scalars and vectors
1. Motion — speed, velocity, acceleration, distance-time and velocity-time graphs
1. Forces and Newton's laws — F=ma, momentum, impulse, conservation of momentum
1. Work, energy and power — W=Fd, kinetic and potential energy, conservation of energy, efficiency
1. Pressure — pressure in solids and liquids, atmospheric pressure, p=F/A and p=ρgh
1. Thermal physics — particle model, specific heat capacity, specific latent heat, conduction, convection and radiation
1. Properties of waves — amplitude, wavelength, frequency, period, the wave equation v=fλ
1. Light — reflection, refraction, total internal reflection, lenses, EM spectrum
1. Sound — longitudinal waves, echoes, ultrasound, Doppler effect
1. Electricity — current, voltage, resistance, Ohm's law, series and parallel circuits, power
1. Magnetism and electromagnetism — magnetic fields, electromagnets, motor effect, Fleming's rules, transformers
1. Atomic physics — nuclear model, radioactive decay, α/β/γ properties, half-life
1. Space physics — solar system, stellar evolution, Big Bang
