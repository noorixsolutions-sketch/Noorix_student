# English Language — 6_AS_Level_Advanced_Stages_10-11

> Advanced study of language in use: text analysis, directed writing, and the language of change.

**Syllabus code:** `9093`  
**Age range:** 16–18  
**Prerequisites:** IGCSE English First Language (Grade B or above).

1. Reading and analysing texts
1. Writing for specific purposes and audiences
1. Language and context — register, mode, genre
1. Language change over time
