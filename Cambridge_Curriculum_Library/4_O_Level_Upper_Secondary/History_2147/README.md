# History — 4_O_Level_Upper_Secondary

> Option-based study of 20th-century world history, including international relations, conflict, and change.

**Syllabus code:** `2147`  
**Age range:** 14–16  
**Prerequisites:** Lower Secondary Humanities.

## Key Topics
1. Was the Treaty of Versailles fair?
1. To what extent was the League of Nations a success?
1. Why had international peace collapsed by 1939?
1. Who was to blame for the Cold War?
1. How effective was the United Nations?
1. Why did the USSR collapse?
1. Choice of depth studies (e.g. China, USA, Germany, Southern Africa)
