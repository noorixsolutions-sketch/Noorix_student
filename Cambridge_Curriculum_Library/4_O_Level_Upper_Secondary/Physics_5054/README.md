# Physics — 4_O_Level_Upper_Secondary

> Introduces classical and modern physics: motion, forces, energy, waves, electricity, magnetism, atomic physics.

**Syllabus code:** `5054`  
**Age range:** 14–16  
**Prerequisites:** Lower Secondary Science with strong mathematical fluency.

## Key Topics
1. Motion, forces, and momentum
1. Energy, work, and power
1. Thermal physics
1. Waves (including sound and light)
1. Electricity and magnetism
1. Atomic physics and radioactivity
1. Space physics (extension topic)
