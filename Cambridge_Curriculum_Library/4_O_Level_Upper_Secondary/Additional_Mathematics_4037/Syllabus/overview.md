## Overview
Stronger emphasis on algebraic manipulation, functions, and calculus than IGCSE Mathematics.

**Age range:** 15–16  
**Prerequisites:** IGCSE Mathematics (or taken concurrently). Designed for high-attaining students.
## Key Topics
- Functions — domain, range, compositions, inverses
- Quadratic functions and discriminant
- Indices, surds, and logarithms
- Simultaneous equations
- Polynomials — factor theorem, remainder theorem
- Trigonometric identities and equations
- Differentiation and integration
- Vectors in 2D
- Kinematics
