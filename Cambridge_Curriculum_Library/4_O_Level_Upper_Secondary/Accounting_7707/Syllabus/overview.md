## Overview
Double-entry bookkeeping, financial statements, and analysis for sole traders, partnerships, and limited companies.

**Age range:** 14–16  
**Prerequisites:** Basic numeracy; complements Business Studies.
## Key Topics
- The accounting equation and double-entry bookkeeping
- Source documents and ledgers
- Trial balance and correction of errors
- Financial statements of sole traders
- Trading and profit and loss accounts
- Balance sheets
- Depreciation and bad debts
- Partnership accounts
- Limited company accounts (intro)
