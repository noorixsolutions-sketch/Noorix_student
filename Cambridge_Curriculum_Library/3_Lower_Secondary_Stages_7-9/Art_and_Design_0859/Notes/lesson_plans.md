### Week 1: Drawing and painting techniques
**Learning Objectives:**
- - Define and explain key terminology related to **Drawing and painting techniques**- - Describe the fundamental principles and concepts of **Drawing and painting techniques**- - Apply understanding of **Drawing and painting techniques** to solve problems and analyse scenarios- - Evaluate and critique different perspectives or approaches within **Drawing and painting techniques**- - Make connections between **Drawing and painting techniques** and other areas of the subject

**Formative Assessment:**
