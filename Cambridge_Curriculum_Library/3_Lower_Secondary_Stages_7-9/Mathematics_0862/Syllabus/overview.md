## Overview
Develops mathematical knowledge, logical reasoning, and problem-solving skills across number, algebra, geometry, statistics, and probability.

**Age range:** 14–16  
**Prerequisites:** Completion of Cambridge Lower Secondary Mathematics (Stage 8) or equivalent.
## Key Topics
- Number, set notation, and language
- Algebra — manipulation, equations, graphs
- Coordinate geometry
- Geometry — angles, triangles, circles, similarity
- Mensuration — area, volume, surface area
- Trigonometry
- Vectors and transformations
- Probability
- Statistics — mean, median, mode, cumulative frequency
