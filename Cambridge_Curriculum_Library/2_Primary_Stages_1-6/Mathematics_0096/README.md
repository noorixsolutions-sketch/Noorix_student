# Mathematics — 2_Primary_Stages_1-6

> Develops mathematical knowledge, logical reasoning, and problem-solving skills across number, algebra, geometry, statistics, and probability.

**Syllabus code:** `0096`  
**Age range:** 14–16  
**Prerequisites:** Completion of Cambridge Lower Secondary Mathematics (Stage 8) or equivalent.

## Key Topics
1. Number, set notation, and language
1. Algebra — manipulation, equations, graphs
1. Coordinate geometry
1. Geometry — angles, triangles, circles, similarity
1. Mensuration — area, volume, surface area
1. Trigonometry
1. Vectors and transformations
1. Probability
1. Statistics — mean, median, mode, cumulative frequency
