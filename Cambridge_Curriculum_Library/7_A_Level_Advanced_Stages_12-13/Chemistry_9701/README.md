# Chemistry — 7_A_Level_Advanced_Stages_12-13

> A Level Chemistry: physical, inorganic and organic chemistry with rigorous quantitative analysis.

**Syllabus code:** `9701`  
**Age range:** 17–18  
**Prerequisites:** AS Level Chemistry (9701) or IGCSE Chemistry.

## Key Topics
1. Atomic structure and electronic configuration — s, p, d notation, ionisation energies
1. Chemical bonding — ionic, covalent, metallic, VSEPR, hybridisation, intermolecular forces
1. States of matter — ideal gas equation, liquid and solid structures
1. Energetics — standard enthalpy changes, Hess's law, Born-Haber cycles, entropy, Gibbs free energy
1. Kinetics — rate laws, mechanisms, rate-determining step, Arrhenius equation
1. Chemical equilibrium — Kc and Kp expressions, Le Chatelier's principle, Haber and Contact processes
1. Acid-base equilibria — Brønsted-Lowry theory, pH, Ka, Kw, buffer solutions, titration curves
1. Electrochemistry — electrode potentials, EMF calculations, electrochemical series, fuel cells
1. Periodicity — Period 3, Group 2, Group 17 chemistry and industrial applications
1. Transition metals — electron configurations, complex ions, colour, catalysis, ligand substitution
1. Organic chemistry — nomenclature, isomerism, halogenoalkanes SN1 and SN2, alcohols, carbonyl compounds
1. Aromatic chemistry — benzene structure, electrophilic substitution mechanisms
1. Polymers — condensation polymerisation, polyesters, polyamides and their hydrolysis
1. Spectroscopy — mass spectrometry, IR and NMR interpretation
