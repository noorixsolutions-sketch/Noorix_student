# Biology — 7_A_Level_Advanced_Stages_12-13

> A Level Biology: biochemistry, cell biology, physiology, genetics, ecology and evolution at university-entrance level.

**Syllabus code:** `9700`  
**Age range:** 17–18  
**Prerequisites:** AS Level Biology (9700) or IGCSE Biology with strong grade.

## Key Topics
1. Cell structure and membranes — ultrastructure, fluid mosaic model, transport mechanisms
1. Biological molecules — carbohydrates, lipids, proteins and enzymes at A Level depth
1. Nucleic acids — DNA structure, semiconservative replication, transcription, translation
1. Cell and nuclear division — mitosis, meiosis and their significance for genetics
1. Transport in plants — xylem structure, cohesion-tension, phloem and mass flow
1. Transport in animals — cardiac cycle, haemoglobin, blood vessels, double circulation
1. Gas exchange — comparison across organisms, alveolar adaptations, counter-current exchange
1. Respiration — glycolysis, Krebs cycle, oxidative phosphorylation and chemiosmosis
1. Photosynthesis — light-dependent reactions, Calvin cycle, limiting factors
1. Homeostasis — osmoregulation by kidney, temperature regulation, blood glucose control
1. Coordination — action potentials, synaptic transmission, hormonal control
1. Genetics and inheritance — Mendel's laws, codominance, sex-linkage, mutation, chi-squared test
1. Evolution and selection — natural selection, speciation, Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium
1. Ecology — ecosystems, nutrient cycles, population ecology, conservation
