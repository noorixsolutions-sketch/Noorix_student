## Revision Techniques
- **Active recall:** test yourself with flashcards (Anki recommended).- **Spaced repetition:** schedule reviews at 1d, 3d, 7d, 21d.- **Past papers:** minimum 5 full past papers under timed conditions.- **Mark schemes:** study them as carefully as the questions.- **Concept maps:** draw relationships between topics.
## Common Pitfalls
- Skipping the basics — every advanced topic rests on foundations.- Memorising without understanding — examiners reward application.- Ignoring command words ('describe' vs 'evaluate' vs 'discuss').- Poor time management in exams — practise under timed conditions.
