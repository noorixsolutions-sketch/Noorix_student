# Law — 7_A_Level_Advanced_Stages_12-13

**Syllabus code:** `9084`  
**Age range:** 16–18  
**Prerequisites:** Strong literacy and analytical thinking.

1. Sources of law and the court system
1. Criminal law — offences, liability, defences
1. Tort law — negligence, occupiers' liability
