# Mathematics — 7_A_Level_Advanced_Stages_12-13

> A Level Mathematics: rigorous pure mathematics including calculus and proof, plus mechanics and statistics.

**Syllabus code:** `9709`  
**Age range:** 17–18  
**Prerequisites:** AS Level Mathematics (9709 Papers 1 & 2) or IGCSE/O Level Mathematics with high grade.

## Key Topics
1. Quadratics and functions — completing the square, discriminant, domain and range, composite and inverse
1. Coordinate geometry — lines, circles, tangents and normals
1. Sequences and series — AP, GP, sum to infinity, binomial theorem
1. Trigonometry — exact values, identities, compound angles, R sin(θ+α) form
1. Differentiation — chain rule, product rule, quotient rule, implicit and parametric
1. Integration — by substitution, by parts, partial fractions, volumes of revolution
1. Differential equations — separable variables, particular solutions
1. Complex numbers — Cartesian and polar forms, De Moivre's theorem
1. Vectors in 3D — scalar product, equations of lines, intersection and angle
1. Mechanics — kinematics, Newton's laws, momentum, moments, energy and power
1. Probability — permutations, combinations, conditional probability
1. Binomial distribution — conditions, mean, variance, hypothesis testing
1. Normal distribution — standardisation, use of tables, normal approximation
