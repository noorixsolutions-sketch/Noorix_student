# Physics — 7_A_Level_Advanced_Stages_12-13

> A Level Physics: classical mechanics, waves, electromagnetism, quantum physics and nuclear physics.

**Syllabus code:** `9702`  
**Age range:** 17–18  
**Prerequisites:** AS Level Physics (9702) or IGCSE Physics with strong mathematical background.

## Key Topics
1. Measurement and units — SI units, errors, uncertainties and dimensional analysis
1. Kinematics and dynamics — projectile motion, Newton's laws, momentum and impulse
1. Work, energy and power — work-energy theorem, conservation of energy, efficiency
1. Matter — stress, strain, Young modulus, Hooke's law
1. Circular motion — centripetal force, angular velocity
1. Gravitational fields — Newton's law of gravitation, field strength, potential and orbital motion
1. Oscillations — SHM, resonance, damping
1. Waves — progressive and stationary waves, interference, diffraction, polarisation
1. Electric fields — Coulomb's law, field strength, potential, capacitors
1. Current electricity — resistivity, Kirchhoff's laws, internal resistance, potential divider
1. Magnetic fields — motor effect, electromagnetic induction, Faraday's law, transformers
1. Quantum physics — photoelectric effect, wave-particle duality, energy levels, de Broglie wavelength
1. Nuclear physics — nuclear structure, binding energy, radioactive decay, nuclear reactions
